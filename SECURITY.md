# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Please report suspected vulnerabilities in kombify Box privately by e-mail to
info@kombify.io, the contact in https://kombify.io/.well-known/security.txt
(policy: https://kombify.io/security).

Do NOT open public issues, pull requests or discussions for security problems.

Include the affected component and version or commit, steps to reproduce (a
minimal proof of concept if possible), the impact you see, and a suggested
mitigation if you have one. Never include live credentials or personal data.

## Response Targets

- Acknowledgement: within 48 hours
- High and Critical severity: fix or mitigation within 14 days
- Lower severity: scheduled into the normal release process

## Disclosure

We coordinate disclosure with the reporter and publish an advisory once a fix
is available.

## Safe Harbour

Good-faith security research is welcome. We will not pursue legal action
against researchers who test only against their own accounts and
installations, do not access, modify or keep data that is not theirs, avoid
privacy violations, data destruction, service disruption and load testing, do
not use social engineering, phishing or physical attacks, and give us a
reasonable chance to fix the issue before public disclosure.

kombify does not run a bug bounty programme.
